## User journey

RF01 exposes one already-created, structurally eligible pending ReflectionRecord from Worldwalker.

Greyson may choose:

- Confirm
- Partial
- Reject
- Uncertain
- Revise
- Private

The UI does not create the Reflection, interpretation, evidence, progression, or provider call.

## Authority behavior

- Confirm may be one tap and does not fabricate response text.
- Partial requires Greyson-authored text and preserves it exactly.
- Reject records rejection without fabricated response.
- Uncertain remains explicitly unresolved.
- Revise requires Greyson-authored replacement text and leaves epistemic status pending for RF05 provenance handling.
- Private marks the Reflection private without changing XP, turns, or evidence.
- M06 structural provenance eligibility controls whether a pending Reflection is offerable.
- While the Reflection dialog is open, Worldwalker/menu interaction is disabled.
- The panel closes when its active Reflection becomes ineligible/resolved.

RF01 does **not** directly call RF02 evidence conversion. Evidence authority remains behind the deterministic Reflection conversion path and its own packet/wiring.

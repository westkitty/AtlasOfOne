## Authority question

Determine whether K03 may safely turn current RF06 contradiction state and RF07 correction-or-change candidates into durable `KnowledgeGap` records under the v2 human-authority and provenance rules.

## Governing sources

### S01 — Product / master integration plan

Governing claims:

- Knowledge selection is deterministic; the model does not decide what Greyson “needs” to examine.
- A `change` gap means recent confirmed material differs materially from older confirmed material.
- PRIVATE/retracted exclusions are structural.
- K03 must accept contradiction/change inputs without drama weighting.
- Greyson has authority over whether something actually changed.

### S02 — D05 domain interface freeze

Current durable `KnowledgeGap` provenance is limited to:

- `sourceEvidenceIds`
- `sourceJournalEntryIds`

D05 requires a lane that cannot implement safely behind the frozen interface to stop and request the minimal contract change.

### S03 — M06 provenance retirement

Current runtime visibility resolves Knowledge Gap provenance only through Evidence and Journal source IDs.

A source-free gap is intentionally considered eligible because coverage-derived unknowns may have no source records.

### S04 — RF07 correction-or-change candidate

RF07 deliberately does **not** claim Greyson changed.

It emits a candidate that distinguishes:

- prior wording: Atlas interpretation;
- replacement wording: Greyson revision;
- `requiresChangeConfirmation: true`.

RF07's source of authority is the Reflection record and its provenance.

## Resolution

### Contradiction input

**IMPLEMENTABLE in principle.**

RF06 contradictions already carry Evidence IDs. A contradiction-derived Knowledge Gap can preserve provenance through existing `sourceEvidenceIds` and can be retired correctly by M06.

### Change input from RF07

**BLOCKED under the current durable interface.**

Two independent failures occur if K03 creates a durable `kind: 'change'` gap today:

1. **Provenance loss**
   - `KnowledgeGap` has no Reflection provenance field.
   - Dropping the source Reflection would make M06 treat the resulting gap as source-free/eligible.
   - PRIVATE/retraction of the Reflection would then fail to retire or withhold the gap structurally.

2. **Human-authority overclaim**
   - RF07 proves only “correction or change needs review.”
   - Converting that candidate directly into `kind: 'change'` would silently answer the unresolved question in favor of “Greyson changed.”
   - That violates the product's human-authority rule.

## Minimal missing interface

K03 requires, at minimum:

### A. Reflection provenance on Knowledge Gaps

A bounded D05/M01-compatible extension such as:

```ts
interface KnowledgeGap {
  // existing fields...
  sourceReflectionIds: string[];
}
```

Requirements:

- default `[]` for existing schema-v2 campaigns;
- M06 must include Reflection visibility in gap support/structural visibility;
- retirement remains ID/provenance-based, never prose-based;
- K05 can then fail closed for private/retracted Reflection-derived gaps.

### B. Explicit human confirmation before durable `kind: 'change'`

A later boundary must distinguish:

- “Atlas was wrong before”; from
- “I actually changed over time.”

K03 must consume a human-authorized result, not RF07's unresolved candidate.

The exact UI/record may be chosen later, but it must preserve:

- the RF07 candidate ID/Reflection ID;
- Greyson's decision;
- provenance to the old/new claims;
- privacy/retraction eligibility.

Until that exists, RF07 candidates may be shown for review but may not become durable `kind: 'change'` Knowledge Gaps.

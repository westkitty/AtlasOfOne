## Eligibility contract

K05 selects only Knowledge Gaps that are:

1. `status:'open'`; and
2. structurally visible according to the merged M06 provenance graph.

This means:

- active normal Journal provenance is eligible;
- PRIVATE Journal provenance is excluded;
- retracted Journal provenance is excluded;
- mixed public/private provenance may remain durable/open under M06's non-destructive retirement rule but is withheld from selection because its stored summary includes ineligible support;
- source-free coverage-derived open gaps remain eligible;
- retired/resolved gaps are not selectable;
- prose content, labels, canary words and topic names have no effect on eligibility.

- Added durable aggregate collections for JournalEntry, KnowledgeGap,
  ReflectionRecord, AdventureSeed/Run/Action/Observation/Memory, AtlasSnapshot,
  CombatDefinition, and optional active CombatState. These are storage only;
  no Journal, Adventure, Combat, or provider runtime was implemented.
- `migrateCampaign` parses source state, deterministically converts v1 to v2
  without randomness or wall-clock input, validates the v2 result, and returns
  it without mutating the source object.
- A legacy FinalAssessment remains in its compatibility field and is also
  preserved as a historical, provider-ineligible AtlasSnapshot carrying the
  original assessment and deterministic source-derived ID.
- The aggregate remains one IndexedDB row (`campaigns: key`); no store/index
  change is needed. A v1 row or v2 retirement-normalization row is parsed and
  migrated in memory, then replaced only after v2 validation succeeds. Failed
  parsing/migration writes nothing.

## Privacy and provenance

- `retirement.ts` uses IDs, record status, and dimensions only: no claim,
  journal, or summary text participates in retirement decisions.
- Private/retracted-only gaps and seeds retire while structural mixed support may
  remain active when an eligible source survives. Prose-bearing memories and
  snapshots use the stricter all-source eligibility rule so a record that may
  contain retired/private source material cannot travel. Historical snapshots
  stay preserved but ineligible; future provider-mode work must consume
  `providerEligibleV2State`, never raw derived v2 collections.
- Adventure observations remain fictional-history records and structurally
  separate from EvidenceRecord.

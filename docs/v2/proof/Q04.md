## Method

Table-driven withdrawal kinds: journal PRIVATE, journal retract, Reflection PRIVATE, Reflection retract, private topic (dimension via `PRIVATE_TOPIC_ADDED` + M06 retirement).

Consumers checked for canary text and quoted withdrawn IDs: `compileContext` + `contextTextFragments`, `selectQualifyingSnapshotProvenance`, `evaluateSnapshotEligibility` request, `synthesizeLocalSnapshot` (new Snapshot), `compareAtlasSnapshots` (old vs new), `explainInsight` (RF08), `eligibleAdventureMemories` / `retrieveEligibleAdventureMemories`, `buildAdventureSeedRequest`; plus M06 visibility predicates and durable status (evidence `retracted`, gap/seed/memory `retired`).

## Contract

RF07 may emit a **revision-review candidate** only from an explicit eligible REVISE provenance delta.

It preserves the unresolved distinction between:

- Atlas was wrong before; and
- Greyson actually changed over time.

The candidate therefore labels:

- prior wording as `atlas-interpretation`;
- replacement wording as `greyson-revision`;
- `requiresChangeConfirmation: true`.

It does not relabel Atlas's old interpretation as Greyson's prior belief and does not invent a `changedAt` timestamp.

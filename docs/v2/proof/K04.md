## Design decision

K01 remains the sole owner of the base Knowledge priority.

K04 applies a transparent **post-score rerank**:

- 80% base relevance contribution;
- 20% bounded novelty/repetition adjustment;
- exact selected gap cooldown across the latest 3 selections;
- structural similarity only from stable territory/theme IDs;
- recency-decayed overlap penalty;
- no gap prose, kind, trauma, pain, vulnerability or drama weighting.

This follows the same separation of relevance from novelty/diversity used by maximal-marginal-relevance style reranking rather than creating a second hidden urgency model.

## Behavior

- exact recently selected gaps rank behind non-cooled alternatives;
- unresolved gaps may return after the short cooldown expires;
- recent structural repetition matters more than older repetition;
- long synthetic selection avoids immediate exact-repeat loops while preserving multiple themes/territories;
- if every candidate is cooling down, Atlas falls back to the strongest base priority instead of returning nothing or compounding punishment;
- non-open gaps are excluded;
- caller-owned gap objects and their K01 priority are never mutated.

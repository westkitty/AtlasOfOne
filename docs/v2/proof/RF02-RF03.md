## Contract decision

RF02 does **not** emit the legacy `EvidenceRecord` or a shared `GameEvent`.

The legacy EvidenceRecord can represent Turn provenance only through `sourceTurnIds`. A Reflection-derived claim may originate from Journal, AdventureObservation, Insight, Contradiction, pattern or Snapshot history. Writing such a claim directly into the legacy shape would either fabricate Turn provenance or silently discard the actual Reflection/source lineage.

RF02 therefore emits a Reflection-owned typed proposal:

- Reflection ID;
- source kind and source IDs;
- explicit Greyson decision;
- epistemic status;
- candidate claim;
- whether that claim came from the explicitly confirmed interpretation or Greyson's own response;
- response text and creation time.

Integration remains responsible for any later shared evidence representation/mutation.

## Authority behavior

A proposal exists only when:

1. the Reflection ID exists;
2. M06 structural provenance visibility says the entire Reflection source path is eligible;
3. the Reflection is active and non-private through that same visibility contract;
4. Greyson supplied a non-empty response;
5. decision/status are either:
   - Confirm + confirmed; or
   - Partial + partial.

Rules:

- Confirm may carry the interpretation text only because Greyson explicitly confirmed it;
- when Confirm has no interpretation, the explicit response becomes the candidate claim;
- Partial never launders the full interpretation: Greyson's response is the candidate claim;
- Reject, Uncertain, Revise, Private, pending, retracted or source-ineligible records produce no proposal;
- PRIVATE/retracted Journal sources fail closed;
- discarded/ineligible AdventureObservation chains fail closed;
- raw AdventureObservation IDs are not accepted at the Reflection conversion boundary;
- output is deliberately not EvidenceRecord/GameEvent-shaped.

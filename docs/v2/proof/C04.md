## Contract implemented

TECHNIQUE remains a small encounter-local tactical system, not a permanent RPG skill tree.

- registry lives on the authored `CombatDefinition`;
- allowed tactical jobs are the five C00 families:
  - interrupt charge;
  - protect ally;
  - expose shield;
  - reposition objective;
  - trade damage for control;
- player starts with the frozen C00 baseline of **2** encounter-local charges;
- technique charge cost is a positive integer and cannot exceed that baseline;
- cooldown is a non-negative integer measured in full combat rounds;
- cooldown 0 means the technique can be used again on the next player round, never repeatedly in the same player phase;
- availability is deterministic through absolute ready-round state;
- activation spends charges and starts cooldown only;
- the returned tactical job is data for later C07-C10 deterministic objective/status mechanics;
- C04 does not itself apply status, objective progress, phase advance, outcome, reward or progression.

## Behavior

### Partial

- requires a structurally eligible Reflection;
- delegates candidate-claim authority to the reviewed RF02 firewall;
- carries Greyson's narrower response as `acceptedClaim`;
- preserves the original interpretation alongside it;
- does not promote the full interpretation.

### Revise

- requires an active structurally eligible Reflection;
- requires non-empty replacement text from Greyson;
- carries the replacement as `replacementClaim`;
- preserves prior source IDs and the original interpretation;
- leaves cross-domain supersession/contestation to later integration-owned logic.

### Fail-closed cases

No provenance delta is produced for:

- Confirm;
- Reject;
- Uncertain;
- Private;
- undecided pending Reflection;
- private/retracted/ineligible source path;
- empty revision response.

Returned source arrays are copies, so consumers cannot mutate stored Reflection history through the proposal object.

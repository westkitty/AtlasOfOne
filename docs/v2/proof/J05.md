## Authority behavior

### PRIVATE

- preserves the exact local Journal record and text;
- sets `privacy:'private'`;
- makes the entry provider-ineligible;
- immediately applies M06 provenance retirement;
- does not imply retraction or delete history.

### Retraction

- preserves the local Journal record and text;
- sets `status:'retracted'`;
- preserves existing privacy state;
- immediately applies M06 provenance retirement;
- does not delete history.

Unknown IDs fail closed.

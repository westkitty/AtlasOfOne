# Atlas runtime art pack — `atlas-v3`

Generated by `tools/art/build_assets.py` and audited by `tools/art/verify_assets.py`.

The build is deterministic: the same sources produce byte-identical output, and
`manifest.json` records a SHA-256 for every file.

## Provenance — be precise about what these are

**The island (`world/`) is generated, not hand-drawn.** `tools/art/world_gen.py`
authors the macro geography by hand — landmasses, ridge lines, basins, river
sources, region anchors, trail waypoints and every landmark are placed
explicitly in code — and uses seeded noise only for coastline irregularity and
terrain texture. Elevation is banded into terraces lit from the north-west,
rivers are traced by steepest descent to the sea, and warm light is pooled at
inhabited places. Seed `20260909`.

`island-hidden`, `island-glimpsed` and `island-revealed` are three exposures of
**one master render**, so a region resolving cannot reveal a different island.
`world/masks/*.png` are soft-Voronoi footprints over that single image, which is
why no polygon boundary is ever visible between territories.

**Greyson (`greyson/`) is a restoration of supplied source art, not new art.**
Source: `atlas_of_one_artpack_v1.zip` and `greyson_runtime_character_v2.zip`,
derived from the canonical Aerron likeness sheets. `tools/art/char_clean.py` keeps
only the connected figure, clears sealed black pockets left by an incomplete
background flood-fill, median-filters the silhouette edge, quantizes every frame
to the canonical skin/hair/hoodie/sherpa/cap/underwear anchors, and translates
frames onto a fixed foot baseline (y=60..63).

**Portraits & Animations:**
Includes 4 emotional portraits (`neutral`, `serious`, `warm`, `wry`) at 96×96,
plus extended animations (`think-front` for sanctuary contemplation, `sit-left` for
resting, `look-up-back` for monolith inspection, `arrive-*` for area arrivals,
`discover-front` for relic discovery, and diagonal walks `walk-qfront-left` / `walk-qback-left`).

`Aerron` is Greyson's former asset code name. Only Aerron-side material from the
supplied packs is canonical; the Andrew-side artwork in those archives is a
different person and is never used.

## Runtime contract

`src/world/manifest.generated.ts` is emitted by the build and is what the
app imports, so coordinates and asset keys are available on first paint with no
fetch and nothing extra to cache offline. Images are ordinary static assets.

Region and trail coordinates live in the manifest, so the app and the art cannot
disagree about where anything is.

## Provenance — be precise about what these are

**The island (`world/`) is generated, not hand-drawn.** `tools/art/world_gen.py`
authors the macro geography by hand — landmasses, ridge lines, basins, river
sources, region anchors, trail waypoints and every landmark are placed
explicitly in code — and uses seeded noise only for coastline irregularity and
terrain texture. Elevation is banded into terraces lit from the north-west,
rivers are traced by steepest descent to the sea, and warm light is pooled at
inhabited places. Seed `20260909`.

`island-hidden`, `island-glimpsed` and `island-revealed` are three exposures of
**one master render**, so a region resolving cannot reveal a different island.
`world/masks/*.png` are soft-Voronoi footprints over that single image, which is
why no polygon boundary is ever visible between territories.

**Greyson (`greyson/`) is a restoration of supplied source art, not new art.**
Source: `greyson_runtime_character_v2.zip`, itself derived from the canonical
Aerron likeness sheets. `tools/art/char_clean.py` keeps only the connected
figure, clears sealed black pockets left by an incomplete background flood-fill,
median-filters the silhouette edge, quantizes every frame to one 44-colour
palette seeded with the canonical skin/hair/hoodie/sherpa/cap/underwear anchors,
and translates — never rescales — frames onto a fixed foot baseline.

**The idle animations are authored, and this matters.** All five idle families in
the source are four byte-identical copies of a single pose. Rather than ship
that as animation, a narrow chest band is lifted by one pixel while the feet and
anchor stay planted. It is a real four-phase breath, but it is derived motion,
not motion that existed in the source.

`Aerron` is Greyson's former asset code name. Only Aerron-side material from the
supplied packs is canonical; the Andrew-side artwork in those archives is a
different person and is never used.

## Runtime contract

`src/world/manifest.generated.ts` is emitted by the same build and is what the
app imports, so coordinates are available on first paint with no fetch and
nothing extra to cache offline. Images are ordinary static assets.

Region and trail coordinates live in the manifest, so the app and the art cannot
disagree about where anything is.
